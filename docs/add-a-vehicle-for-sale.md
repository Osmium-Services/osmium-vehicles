**Vehicles** lists the cars, caravans, motorhomes or vans you have for sale, each published as its own page.

## Add a vehicle

Press to add a vehicle. A short guide takes you through four steps.

1. **Vehicle:** enter the **Make** and **Model** (both required), and optionally the **Year**, **Price**, **Mileage** and **Registration**.
2. **Photos:** choose photos or drop them in. On a phone you can take them with the camera. Drag to reorder, and the first photo is the cover.
3. **Details:** write the **Description**, choose the **Fuel** and **Transmission**, enter the **Engine**, and list the **Features**, one per line.
4. **Publish:** choose when it goes on the website.

## Edit a vehicle

Open it to use the tabs: **Page**, **Details**, **Photos**, **Description** and **Specs**. Use **Add feature** to add features, and **Show on the website** under Publish to show or hide the vehicle. **View on Site** opens its page. Press **Save Changes** when done.
